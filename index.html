A LETTER FROM MY HEART

Dear Nadra,

Six years… When I think about that number, I don't just think about the time that has passed. I think about everything we have been through together. The beautiful moments, the stupid arguments, the misunderstandings, the endless conversations, the days when everything felt perfect, and the days when things felt like they were falling apart.

Our journey was never perfect, and honestly, I never wanted it to be. Because somewhere between all those problems and difficult moments, we learned what it actually means to love someone.

We have had our share of fights. We have misunderstood each other, hurt each other, got angry, stopped talking, and sometimes wondered if things would ever become normal again. There were moments when being together wasn't easy. There were countless things that could have pulled us apart, countless reasons to give up, and countless situations where walking away would have been easier.

But somehow, we stayed.

We chose to talk again. We chose to forgive. We chose to understand each other. We chose to give our relationship another chance, again and again. And I think that's what makes our story so special to me.

Six years later, I don't love you because everything between us has always been perfect. I love you because even when things weren't perfect, there was always a part of us that still wanted to make things work.

We've grown so much during these years. We aren't the same people we were when this journey started. We've changed, matured, learned from our mistakes, and experienced so many things together. And through all those changes, one thing somehow remained constant — us.

There are memories with you that I could never explain properly with words. The random conversations, the silly jokes, the little moments, the times we laughed for absolutely no reason, and even the moments when we were angry at each other but secretly still cared. All of them became pieces of a story that I wouldn't replace with anything.

I know we still have a long way to go. There will probably be more disagreements, more difficult days, and moments when we won't understand each other. But after everything we've already survived, I know that what we have is stronger than just the easy days.

I don't want a relationship where we never fight or never face problems. I want a relationship where, despite everything, we keep finding our way back to each other.

And if there is one thing these six years have taught me, it's that love isn't just about staying when everything is beautiful. Love is also about staying when things get difficult, learning, forgiving, growing, and choosing the same person even after seeing their imperfections.

So on your birthday, I don't just want to wish you happiness. I want to thank you.

Thank you for staying. Thank you for the memories. Thank you for every laugh, every conversation, every argument, every apology, every second of your time, and every little thing you've added to my life.

Thank you for being a part of six of the most meaningful years of my life.

I don't know exactly what the future has planned for us, but I do know that when I look back at these six years, I will always be grateful that it was you standing beside me.

Happy Birthday, my love. ❤️

Here's to everything we've been through, everything we've overcome, everything we've learned, and all the beautiful memories that are still waiting for us.

Six years down… and hopefully, a lifetime to go.

I love you. ❤️

Always,
Rameez
